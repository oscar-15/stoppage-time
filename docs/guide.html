Stoppage Time

A one-page cheat sheet. The short version: the site finds spots where a US sportsbook is paying more than a game is really worth.

The idea in 30 seconds

Pinnacle is the sharpest sportsbook in the world. It takes huge bets from professionals and keeps a tiny margin, so its odds, with that margin removed, are the best estimate of each team's true chance. We call those the fair odds.

US books (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and others) usually pay less than fair. Every so often one pays more. That's a value bet, and that's all this site looks for.

Odds refresher

  • +150Bet $100 to win $150. Underdog.
  • −150Bet $150 to win $100. Favorite.
  • Better for youThe bigger number pays more: +130 beats +120, and −110 beats −120.

The one rule: if the US price is bigger than the fair price, it's a value bet. If it's smaller, skip it.

The example's numbers are made up to show a green box. xG 1.8–1.5 is expected goals: on average, the market expects Como to score about 1.8 and United about 1.5.

Picks and leans

  • PickA US price beats fair by 2% or more. Up to 10 a day, one per match.
  • LeanA smaller edge, 0.5–2%. Listed under the picks, tracked on its own record.
  • "True odds" barThe real chance it hits.
  • "Book pays" barThe chance the US price implies. When it's shorter than the true-odds bar, you're getting paid extra.
  • Pinnacle (derived)Fair price worked out from Pinnacle's nearby line, so it needs 3% instead of 2%.

Picks never change once posted, they lock at kickoff, and they grade themselves from the final score.

Arbitrage (orange box)

Rarely, books disagree so much that betting every outcome, each at a different book, guarantees a profit no matter who wins. The orange box lists each leg and how much to put on it per $100. Place all legs or none, and check every price first: these disappear in minutes. They're separate from the record.

Is it working? Watch CLV

Closing-line value (CLV) compares the price we posted with Pinnacle's last price before kickoff. If we keep getting better prices than the close, the edge is real, even through losing streaks.

  • Good signAverage CLV above 0, and "beat the close" over 55–60%, after 50–100 picks.
  • Win-lossTakes hundreds of bets to mean anything. Don't judge it on a week.

House rules

Bet small and flat (the same amount every time). Never chase losses. Prices move, so check the book's current price before betting: if it's dropped below fair, skip it. Only bet where it's legal for you.
